## My role

I led the education program: finding where organizations stalled, designing and producing the guidance across video, Academy, and in-app, and analyzing the results.

## The problem

Embedded Studio lets nonprofits place a donation form on their own website. Interest was strong, but only about a third of the organizations that created an embedded campaign went on to take a donation.

The drop-off came after publishing. To install the form, admins had to edit code on their own website — something many had never done.

## What I did

- **Found the stall point.** Usage data showed organizations publishing and then stopping before installation. The barrier was a skill gap, not a product bug.
- **Taught code editing at scale.** I prioritized the three most common website builders — WordPress, Squarespace, and Wix — and made a video for each, an Academy walkthrough, and in-app walkthroughs using GIFs cut from those videos. One production effort fed three channels.
- **Put help at the moment of need.** The in-app guides appeared right after publishing, when installation was the admin's next task. A launcher on the Install page let admins pick their website builder and start the matching walkthrough at any time.
- **Measured activation, not clicks.** I compared organizations that used a guide with those that didn't, on the outcome that mattered: their first donation.

## Results

Over a 90-day window:

- Organizations that used at least one guide were **about twice as likely to start taking donations** (roughly half, versus about a fifth).
- Their median time to first donation was **about 4 days faster** (12 days down to 8).
- **About 3 in 4** organizations that published used at least one guide.
- Short, single-task guides performed best. The Wix guide reached few organizations with no clear benefit — a reminder to keep checking reach after launch.

This compares organizations that chose to use guides with those that didn't, so motivated organizations may be overrepresented. It's a strong association, not proof of cause.

## What I learned

Timing beat volume. Help that arrived right after publishing — when the admin's next job was installing — did more than any broad announcement could.
