Giving season is the most critical period for nonprofit fundraising. In 2026, I led a cross-channel education program to help nonprofit organizations adopt the tools they needed — Giving Cart, recurring giving, donor segmentation, QR codes — before their busiest fundraising window.

This wasn't just course production. I diagnosed customer barriers, chose the smallest useful interventions across Academy courses, in-app guidance, and tools, coordinated three coaches, and built measurement into the program from the start.

## Challenge

GoFundMe Pro had shipped several high-impact features for giving season, but adoption lagged behind availability:

- **Feature awareness gaps:** Organizations didn't know tools like Giving Cart or Smart Retries existed, or didn't understand how they applied to their fundraising.
- **No unified education path:** Product announcements, help articles, and Academy courses existed in silos. A nonprofit preparing for giving season had no single place to learn what they needed.
- **No adoption measurement:** We could track whether content was published and whether people viewed it, but we had no framework connecting education to feature activation.

## Solution

I designed a multi-format education program with defined learning paths and a measurement framework.

### Curriculum

I shipped 5 Academy courses in a single quarter, each targeting a specific giving season capability:

- **Giving Cart** — how to enable and promote the new multi-cause donation experience
- **Mobile optimization** — preparing campaigns for mobile donors during peak traffic
- **Donor segmentation** — using data to target outreach to the right supporters
- **QR codes and direct mail** — bridging print and digital fundraising with tracked links
- **Campaign spotlights** — featuring campaigns to increase visibility during giving season

Each course included defined learning objectives and completion tracking through Skilljar.

### Supporting content

- **Giving season hub** in the Academy library: 16 existing resources and 4 highlights, organized with a carousel for discoverability
- **Partner webinar series** page with RKD Group, Creative Shizzle, and Revunami — published to extend reach beyond our own channels
- **In-app guidance:** templates embedded on the Home dashboard and a Campaigns tooltip to surface relevant tools in context

### Campaign URL builder with QR codes

I extended a live Academy tool so organizations could create tracked links and download print-ready QR codes. Built with an embedded JavaScript library (`qrcode-generator`), it supports print-size exports, accessible status messages, and malformed-host rejection. Published alongside the QR codes and direct mail course so the tool and the education content reinforced each other.

### Measurement

I drafted a pilot scorecard to evaluate whether education content drove product adoption:

- **Primary metric:** eligible organizations exposed to giving season content who activated a tracked feature (Giving Cart, Smart Retries, recurring options) within 30 days
- **Supporting metrics:** course enrollments and completions, webinar registrations, template starts, in-app guide engagement

This was the first time our team proposed measuring education against product outcomes rather than content engagement alone.

## Results

- **5 courses** published on schedule before the giving season window
- **Smart Retries:** 252 enrolled organizations within one week of launch, including 80 beta migrations
- Pilot scorecard framework established for tying education to adoption

## What I learned

The biggest shift was moving from "did people watch this" to "did people do something after learning this." The pilot scorecard was imperfect — we couldn't fully isolate the effect of education from other factors — but framing the question changed how the team thought about what to build next. We stopped planning content by feature launch date and started planning by customer barrier.
